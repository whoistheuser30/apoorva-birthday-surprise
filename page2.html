The Morning Photo Case

Some memories become special because they were planned. This one became memorable because absolutely nobody planned what happened next.

EXHIBIT B

The photo behind one very unexpected investigation.

INCIDENT REPORT

It started with one simple request.

One morning, I asked Apoorva to send me an early-morning photo. She sent one with a big smile showing her teeth.

Normally, that would have been the end of the story.

Unfortunately for both of us, my mom happened to see it.

My mom laughed and immediately asked me: “Who is she?”

Which made the situation even funnier because my mom has always been pretty strict about me talking to girls.

Later, I told Apoorva what happened and we both laughed about my mom's reaction.

Somehow, one ordinary morning photo became one of those little inside jokes that stayed in my memory.

INVESTIGATOR NOTES

Findings from the case

One requested morning picture. Nothing suspicious yet.

My mom saw the photo before the investigation could be contained.

Final Outcome

No emergency. Just two people laughing about the whole situation.
