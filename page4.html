OCTOBER 30 • FINAL BIRTHDAY FILE

A Letter for Apoorva

No investigation this time. No questions to answer. Just a few things I wanted to write down for your birthday.

October 30

Dear Apoorva,

Happy Birthday.

When I think about how long we have known each other, it is strange how many memories started from something as simple as Facebook messages.

We do not talk every day, and life has changed around us in many ways, but I have always been genuinely happy whenever we talk.

Sometimes it is a long call. Sometimes it is a random message. Sometimes it is just one small memory that suddenly comes back. Somehow, all of those little things became part of a friendship that I am grateful for.

I admire how straightforward and bold you are. You work hard, you try to stand on your own, and the way you care about your parents says a lot about the kind of person you are.

You can be talkative, emotional, strong, caring and completely yourself, sometimes all at once. And yes, you also have a smile that has caused at least one unexpected investigation at my house.

I know life has its own paths, relationships and priorities, and I respect yours. This page is not here to ask you for anything. I simply wanted your birthday to have something made with care, built from memories that genuinely mean something to me.

I hope this year brings you peace when you need it, confidence when things feel uncertain, success in the things you work hard for, and more reasons to laugh without having to explain yourself.

I hope you keep choosing what makes you happy, keep growing into the person you want to become, and never lose the bold and caring side of yourself that makes you who you are.

Whatever direction life takes, I am grateful for the friendship, the conversations, the ridiculous little memories, and the chance to have known you through all these years.

Have a beautiful birthday, Apoorva. I genuinely hope October 30 starts a really good year for you.

Happy Birthday 🎂 With lots of good wishes
